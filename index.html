⚽ About Us • Lagos & Osun State, Nigeria Elite Youth Football Institution

About Us: Simolat Football Academy

Simolat Football Academy is an elite, player-centered football institution based in Lagos and Osun State, Nigeria. We are dedicated to identifying, nurturing, and developing the next generation of football champions by combining world-class coaching with a holistic approach to personal growth.

Our Philosophy

At Simolat, the player comes first. We believe that true athletic excellence happens when young athletes are given the right environment to thrive both on and off the pitch. Our training programs are tailored to each individual's strengths, helping players build technical mastery, tactical intelligence, physical resilience, and mental toughness.

What Sets Us Apart

Player-Centered Development

Every training session, mentorship program, and performance review is designed around the unique needs and career goals of our athletes.

Elite Coaching Standards

Our certified coaching staff brings modern football methodologies to Lagos and Osun, bridging the gap between grassroots talent and professional standards.

Character & Education

We develop more than just footballers. Discipline, leadership, teamwork, and academic accountability are core pillars of life at Simolat.

Pathways to Success

Through competitive showcase matches, scouting networks, and structured career guidance, we prepare our players for local and international opportunities.

Our Vision

To stand as West Africa's premier hub for youth football excellence, empowering young talents in Lagos and Osun State to reach the highest levels of the global game while becoming role models in their communities.

Join the Journey

Whether you are a young athlete dreaming of a professional career or a parent looking for a supportive, high-standard environment for your child, Simolat Football Academy is where potential meets opportunity.

BUILDING CONFIDENCE, CHARACTER & COMMUNITY

At Simolat Football Academy, football is a vehicle for lifelong personal growth. Through structured mentorship, peer accountability, community outreach, and disciplined daily habits across our Lagos and Osun State campuses, every player learns to lead with humility, communicate effectively under pressure, and represent their family and community with pride both on the pitch and in the classroom.

🛡️ Child & Youth Protection Charter • Nigeria & FIFA Guardians Aligned

SIMOLAT FOOTBALL ACADEMY SAFEGUARDING POLICY (AGES 9–21)

Formal Safeguarding & Welfare Framework for Male and Female Academy Players Aged 9 to 21 — Aligned with the Nigerian Child's Rights Act (2003), Lagos State Child's Rights Law (2015) and Osun State Child's Right Law. Violence Against Persons (Prohibition) Act (2015), FIFA Guardians, and CAF Youth Protection Standards.

1. Policy Statement & Commitment

  • Simolat Football Academy enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy toward all forms of abuse, exploitation, neglect, discrimination, or harassment against any player aged 9 to 21.
  • Every player has the fundamental right to train, compete, and develop in a safe, supportive environment where their welfare is paramount above sporting results or commercial interests.
  • In accordance with the Nigerian Child's Rights Act (2003) and Lagos State Child's Rights Law, the best interests of the child and young person shall be the primary consideration in all academy decisions.

2. Scope & Age-Specific Considerations

  • Foundation Phase (Ages 9–12): Mandatory parental/guardian drop-off and collection protocols; heightened emotional reassurance; age-appropriate coaching language; strict prohibition on punitive physical drills.
  • Youth Development Phase (Ages 13–17): Recognized as minors under Nigerian law; mandatory parental consent for all medical care, travel, and media; protection against academic neglect, peer bullying, and premature physical overload.
  • Professional Development Phase / Young Adults (Ages 18–21): Recognizing “adults at risk” and inherent power imbalances between young players seeking professional contracts and coaching, medical, or scouting staff; strict prohibition on coercion, financial exploitation, or abuse of authority under the VAPP Act (2015).

3. Definitions of Abuse & Harm

  • Physical Abuse: Any non-accidental physical harm, corporal punishment, excessive endurance drills used as punishment, or forcing an injured player to train/compete.
  • Emotional Abuse: Persistent verbal humiliation, shouting, threats, body-shaming, or psychological manipulation that damages a player's self-worth.
  • Sexual Abuse & Exploitation: Any sexualized comment, grooming, inappropriate touching, or sexual act involving a minor or young adult where consent is compromised by authority or coercion.
  • Neglect: Failure to provide adequate hydration, medical attention, safe equipment, supervision, or safeguarding oversight.
  • Bullying, Hazing & Peer-on-Peer Abuse: Any initiation ritual, physical intimidation, exclusion, or cyber-harassment committed by teammates or older squad members.

4. Code of Conduct for Staff & Volunteers

  • Physical Contact: Only permitted when strictly necessary for technical demonstration (with verbal explanation first) or emergency first aid; never in private.
  • One-on-One Coaching: Unmonitored private 1-on-1 sessions are strictly prohibited; all individual coaching must occur in open, visible areas with another vetted adult present.
  • Changing Rooms: Adults must never enter changing rooms while players are undressing; separate changing facilities and schedules must be maintained for male, female, and adult vs. minor squads.
  • Medical & Physio Treatments: All physiotherapy or medical examinations for players under 18 require a chaperone (parent/guardian or second vetted staff member) and open-door visibility.
  • Background Vetting: Mandatory pre-employment criminal record screening (Nigeria Police Character Certificate / international DBS-equivalent) and reference checks before any staff or volunteer interacts with players.

5. Digital Safeguarding & Social Media

  • WhatsApp & Team Messaging: Staff must never send private direct messages (DMs) to players under 18. Official team WhatsApp groups must include parents/guardians and the Designated Safeguarding Officer.
  • Photography & Video Analysis: Written parental/player consent is required before capturing or publishing match/training photos or performance videos; all match footage must be stored securely on academy-owned encrypted systems, never on personal devices.
  • Social Media Boundaries: Staff are prohibited from following, friending, or engaging in private online chats with minor players on Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, or similar platforms.

6. Travel, Tournaments & Accommodation

  • Overnight Stays & Rooming: Players must never share bedrooms or beds with coaches, staff, or adults. Male and female players must be housed in separate, secure quarters, and minors must be grouped strictly by age bracket.
  • Medical Consent: Written parental/guardian medical & emergency treatment consent forms and emergency contact details must travel with the team medic for every away fixture.
  • Supervision Ratios: Minimum ratio of 1 vetted adult per 8 players for Foundation Phase (Ages 9–12) and 1 vetted adult per 10 players for Youth Development (Ages 13–17), including female chaperones for female squads.

7. Reporting & Whistleblowing Procedure

  • Step 1 — Immediate Safety: Ensure the player is safe and, if injured or in immediate danger, contact emergency medical services or the Nigeria Police Family Support Unit.
  • Step 2 — Report Within 24 Hours: Players, parents, or staff must report any concern directly to the Designated Safeguarding Officer (DSO) in person, by phone, or via confidential email.
  • Step 3 — Documentation & Confidentiality: The DSO records factual details securely on a need-to-know basis. Information is shared only with statutory authorities (Lagos State Domestic and Sexual Violence Agency [DSVA], NAPTIP, or Police) as required by Nigerian law.
  • Whistleblower Protection: Anyone reporting a concern in good faith is strictly protected against retaliation, intimidation, loss of squad place, or contract termination.
